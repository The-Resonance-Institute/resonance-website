Three things happen, and only the first and last are a model. A small model
    reads your sentence into nine fields. A deterministic gate judges those nine and composes the
    package — no model anywhere in that step. Then a second model answers you with that package
    as its instructions. Send the same reading twice and you get the same judgment, always.

    
The two digests are the part worth checking. One is taken over the package shown above, the
    other over what was actually delivered to the answering model. They match, so the text you are
    reading is the text that was sent — not a description of it written afterwards.

    
How reliably a model turns a sentence into those nine fields is a question we are still
    measuring and have not answered. That is why the reading is shown to you rather than hidden: you
    can see what it saw, and disagree with it.
